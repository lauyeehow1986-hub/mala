# Singapore Mala: Multi-Quantum Bang-for-Bucks ($10 to $30 SGD) Arbitrage Report

## Executive Summary
This report presents the exact quantitative knapsack optimizations across five budget quanta (**$\$10.00$**, **$\$15.00$**, **$\$20.00$**, **$\$25.00$**, and **$\$30.00$ SGD**) under Singapore restaurant pricing architectures:
- **Unified Weight Chains** ($\$2.98/100\text{g}$, e.g. *Yang Guo Fu, Zhang Liang, Gong Yuan, A Hot Hideout*)
- **Tiered Weight Stalls** ($\$1.50\text{--}\$3.50/100\text{g}$, e.g. *Food Republic, Koufu, Wan Xing*)
- **Fixed Per-Portion Hawkers** ($\$1.00\text{--}\$3.00/\text{portion}$, e.g. *Ri Ri Hong @ People's Park Complex, Labula*)

All items are benchmarked against verified Singapore retail prices (NTUC FairPrice, Sheng Siong, RedMart) and wholesale suppliers (Pasir Panjang Wholesale Centre, Victoria Wholesale Centre, Song Fish Dealer, Fassler Gourmet).

## 3. The 4 Rules of Mala Value Arbitrage

1. **Avoid Pre-Soaked Water Traps at Unified Chains**: Sweet potato wide vermicelli (*kuan fen*), mung bean vermicelli (*tang hoon*), and wood ear fungus absorb $4.5\times\text{--}5.2\times$ water weight. At $\$2.98/100\text{g}$, effective stall COGS is $\$0.06\text{--}\$0.09/100\text{g}$ ($+98\%$ stall margin!).
2. **Exploit Expansion Items**: Dry ring rolls (*ling ling juan*), dry fried tau kee, and dry instant noodles weigh minimal grams on the scale but expand $3\times\text{--}4\times$ during cooking.
3. **Route Vegetables to Fixed Per-Portion Hawkers**: Order heavy tubers (lotus root, potato, radish) and bulky vegetables at *Ri Ri Hong* or *Labula*, where $\$1.00$ buys a massive $180\text{g}$ heap ($\sim \$0.55/100\text{g}$ vs $\$2.98/100\text{g}$ at chains).
4. **Vigorously Shake the Colander**: Drainage of trapped water before weighing saves $40\text{--}60\text{g}$ ($+\$1.20\text{--}\$1.80$ in water tax).
